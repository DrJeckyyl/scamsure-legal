Delete your ScamSure account

ScamSure allows you to permanently delete your account and associated ScamSure data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to ScamSure.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the confirmation message and select Delete account again.

When in-app deletion succeeds, your ScamSure authentication account is permanently deleted and the ScamSure data stored locally for that account is removed from that device.

Request deletion without access to the app

If you cannot access ScamSure, email trochimchukmark@gmail.com and request deletion of your ScamSure account.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ScamSure account so account ownership can be verified. Never send your password, recovery codes, financial information, or identification documents.

What is deleted

  • Your ScamSure authentication account.
  • Account metadata associated with that authentication account.
ScamSure scan contents and scan history are stored locally on your device, not in ScamSure's server database. If you request account deletion by email instead of using the in-app deletion option, remove remaining local data by clearing ScamSure's app storage or uninstalling the app from that device.
