ScamSure Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScamSure accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use the ScamSure mobile application.

1. Information used for your ScamSure account

When you create a ScamSure account, ScamSure uses Supabase Auth to process the following information:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your account password or updated password credentials.
  • Authentication and session information needed to keep you signed in.

Password authentication is handled by Supabase. ScamSure does not keep plaintext passwords in its own application database.

2. Authentication and technical security information

Supabase provides ScamSure's authentication infrastructure. As part of operating and securing that service, Supabase may process technical and authentication information such as login and signup events, password changes, account-deletion events, timestamps, IP addresses, user-agent information, request metadata, and related security logs.

This information is used to provide authentication, maintain service security, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, and operate the authentication service.

3. Scam scans, screenshots, photos, QR codes and OCR

ScamSure is designed so that scam-analysis content remains on your device.

ScamSure may allow you to type or paste suspicious text, select a screenshot, photograph suspicious content, or scan a QR code.

  • Screenshot and photo text recognition is performed on-device.
  • ScamSure uses on-device ML Kit text recognition for OCR.
  • QR-code scanning is performed using the device camera.
  • ScamSure does not upload your scam screenshots or photographs to its backend.
  • ScamSure does not upload your scan text or scan history to its backend.

4. Information stored locally on your device

ScamSure stores certain application data locally on your device, including:

  • Scam scan history.
  • Saved items or alerts.
  • Guide-read status.
  • Application settings.
  • Authentication session information used to maintain sign-in.

ScamSure's local application data is separated by authenticated account so one account does not load another account's ScamSure scan history.

5. Device permissions

ScamSure may use the following device capabilities when needed:

  • Camera: for QR-code scanning and photographing suspicious content when you choose to use those features.
  • Network access: for ScamSure account authentication and related Supabase services.
  • Vibration/haptic feedback: for interface feedback.

ScamSure does not request location permission and does not use a draw-over-other-apps permission.

6. How information is used

ScamSure uses information described in this policy to:

  • Create and authenticate ScamSure accounts.
  • Maintain signed-in sessions.
  • Allow users to update their profile name or password.
  • Provide ScamSure's fraud and scam-analysis features.
  • Store user-selected application information locally on the device.
  • Protect account and service security.
  • Process account-deletion requests.

7. Google Play subscriptions and purchases

ScamSure offers an optional "ScamSure Pro" subscription through Google Play's billing system.

  • Purchases are made directly through Google Play. ScamSure never receives, processes, or stores your payment card number, bank details, or other full payment credentials — those are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • When you purchase or restore a subscription, your Google Play purchase token and the resulting subscription status (for example, active, cancelled, or expired) are sent to ScamSure's backend (Supabase) so it can verify the purchase with the Google Play Developer API and record whether your account is entitled to ScamSure Pro.
  • This subscription/entitlement record is linked to your ScamSure account and is used only to determine whether ScamSure Pro features are unlocked for that account.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from Google Play's own subscription-management screen, reachable from ScamSure's Account tab.

8. Analytics and advertising

The current version of ScamSure does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

9. Service providers and sharing

ScamSure uses Supabase as a service provider for account authentication and related backend infrastructure, including verifying and recording ScamSure Pro subscription status (Section 7). Information needed for authentication and security is therefore processed by Supabase on ScamSure's behalf.

ScamSure also uses Google Play to process subscription purchases and to confirm subscription status via the Google Play Developer API. Google Play's own privacy policy governs the payment information Google Play itself collects.

ScamSure does not sell users' personal information or scam-analysis content.

Information may also be disclosed if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, ScamSure, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

10. Data security

ScamSure uses HTTPS/TLS connections when communicating with Supabase. Sensitive server administrative credentials used for account deletion are kept in the server-side Supabase environment and are not included in the ScamSure mobile application.

ScamSure also reduces exposure of scam-analysis content by processing screenshot OCR on-device and storing scan history locally rather than uploading it to the ScamSure backend.

11. Data retention

ScamSure account information is retained while the account exists and may be deleted when the user deletes the account or submits a valid deletion request.

Subscription/entitlement records (Section 7) are retained while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your ScamSure account, the same as the account information above.

Locally stored ScamSure data remains on the user's device until it is deleted through ScamSure, the account is deleted through the app, application storage is cleared, or the app is removed from the device.

Supabase may retain operational, authentication, security, and service logs according to its service configuration and retention practices. Limited information may also be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

12. Account and data deletion

Users can permanently delete their ScamSure account from within the app:

  1. Sign in to ScamSure.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Complete the confirmation process.

When in-app account deletion succeeds, ScamSure deletes the user's authentication account and removes that account's locally stored ScamSure application data from that device.

ScamSure also provides a public account-deletion page: ScamSure Account Deletion .

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing trochimchukmark@gmail.com from the email address associated with your ScamSure account.

13. Privacy Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ScamSure's features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
